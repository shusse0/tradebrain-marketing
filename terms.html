4. Service Description and Limitations

4.1 Architecture

TradeBrain uses proprietary classification algorithms and analytical models developed by Defense Trade Solutions to determine commodity classifications under the Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS) for imports and Schedule B for exports. The platform incorporates artificial intelligence services for natural language processing and product analysis as one component within a multi-layered classification engine.

All classification logic — including GRI (General Rules of Interpretation) analysis, Section and Chapter Note enforcement, candidate code generation, heading-level discrimination, confidence scoring, and regulatory compliance checks — is performed by the TradeBrain proprietary engine. AI services process product descriptions and return structured data which is then validated, scored, and refined by the TradeBrain classification brain before any result is presented to the user.

4.2 Decision-Support Tool

TradeBrain is a decision-support tool designed to assist trade compliance professionals with commodity classification analysis. Classifications provided by TradeBrain are advisory in nature and represent the platform's analytical determination based on the information provided by the user.

The user, as the importer or exporter of record, retains ultimate responsibility for all classification decisions, customs filings, export license determinations, and compliance with applicable laws and regulations. TradeBrain classifications should be reviewed by qualified trade compliance personnel before use in official filings or regulatory submissions.

4.3 Accuracy and Continuous Improvement

TradeBrain's classification engine is continuously tested, validated, and improved through rigorous quality assurance processes. While the platform achieves high accuracy rates across commodity categories, certain products may present classification ambiguities where multiple tariff headings could reasonably apply under the General Rules of Interpretation. In such cases, TradeBrain presents its analytical determination along with supporting rationale, enabling the user to make an informed final decision.

4.4 Data Dependency

Classification accuracy depends on the quality and completeness of information provided by the user. Vague, incomplete, or ambiguous product descriptions may result in lower-confidence classifications. Users are encouraged to provide detailed product descriptions, technical specifications, and supporting documentation to achieve the most accurate classification results.
